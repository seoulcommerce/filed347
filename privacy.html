Privacy

Filed347. Not a legal opinion. We do not file for you. We do not say you are compliant.

Who this is for

Public-works subcontractors generating a WH-347. Not the public. Not employees.

What we ask for

Contractor and project fields you type. A payroll CSV. Optional certifying-official name, title, phone, and email. An email only if you later subscribe. Prime or subcontractor, and whether this is the final payroll.

No full SSN

Last 4 only. We drop anything that looks like a 9-digit SSN. Do not upload a full Social Security number. Identifying numbers on the PDF are last four characters only.

How long data lives

The CSV is used to generate the PDF. It is not kept as a payroll archive. The raw CSV is stripped from the stored record.

The preview payload (parsed fields and worker rows without the raw CSV) and the generated PDF are held in server memory for 24 hours, then gone. After 24 hours the id no longer downloads.

sessionStorage in your browser is only a fallback if the server id is lost. Closing the tab clears that copy.

Email

Contact: Filed347. We do not accept payroll CSVs by email. Do not send payroll files, employee names, or last-4s by email. There is no payroll inbox.
