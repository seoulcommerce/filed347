Official form

WH-347 is the Department of Labor's optional weekly certified payroll form for Davis-Bacon and Related Acts work. Instructions and the blank form live on dol.gov, not here:

WH-347 on dol.gov

Use of that specific form is optional. Submitting weekly certified payroll is not. We generate a WH-347-style PDF from your file. We are not DOL.

Privacy

Who this is for. Public-works subcontractors generating a WH-347. Not the public. Not employees.

What we ask for. Contractor and project fields you type. A payroll CSV. Optional certifying-official name, title, phone, and email. Prime or subcontractor, and whether this is the final payroll.

No full SSN. Last 4 only. We drop anything that looks like a 9-digit SSN. Do not upload a full Social Security number. Identifying numbers on the PDF are last four characters only.

How long data lives. The CSV is used to generate the PDF. It is not kept as a payroll archive. The raw CSV is stripped from the stored record. The preview payload (parsed fields and worker rows without the raw CSV) and the generated PDF are held in server memory for 24 hours, then gone. After 24 hours the id no longer downloads. sessionStorage in your browser is only a fallback if the server id is lost. Closing the tab clears that copy.

Email. Do not send payroll files, employee names, or last-4s by email. There is no payroll inbox.

Not a law firm. This is software that fills a form from your file. Not a legal opinion. We do not file for you. We do not say you are compliant.
