What it does

  • You export a week of payroll to CSV. We print a WH-347 payroll table and the Statement of Compliance wording.
  • Certification boxes are printed as text and left unmarked. Signature lines stay blank.
  • Wage determination is printed only as you pasted it. We do not invent a WD number.
  • Last four of the employee id only. Never a full SSN.
  • $69 a month when checkout is live. Not Knowify. Not a lawyer.

What you still do

Review the PDF. Mark the boxes that apply (prime / sub / final payroll if they are on the form). Sign it. File it with the GC. We do not file it. We do not say you are compliant.

What it is not

  • Not Knowify. We do not run the job or the back office.
  • Not a lawyer. Not a law firm. Not a legal opinion.
  • Not LCPtracker, eBacon, or a DIR portal. If your GC only takes those, this PDF may be unused.
  • Not a blank form to retype. The point is the table comes from the file.

How it works

  1. Look at the sample PDF and the sample CSV.

  3. Paste the project fields you already have, including the WD from the contract.

Privacy

Who this is for. Public-works subcontractors filing a WH-347. Not the public. Not employees.

What we ask for. Contractor and project fields you type. A payroll CSV. Optional certifying-official name, title, phone, email. A billing email for Stripe when checkout is live.

SSN. Last four only. Do not upload a full Social Security number. If a cell looks longer than four digits, we keep the last four.

How long the CSV lives.

  • The CSV is read in your browser.
  • It sits in your browser session until you download the PDF or close the tab.
  • The server sees the CSV only in the request that builds the PDF. That request is not written to a payroll database or a disk folder in this product.
  • After that request ends, we do not keep the CSV.
  • If you close the tab before download, the copy in your browser is gone. We cannot rebuild it from email.

Email. Do not send payroll files, employee names, or last-4s to any Seoul Web Design or seoulwebdesign.com address. There is no payroll inbox. Payroll data does not belong in email.

Not a law firm. This is software that fills a form from your file.
